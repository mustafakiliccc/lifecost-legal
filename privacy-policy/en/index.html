Receipt scanning (OCR): performed entirely on your device using Google ML Kit. No image or recognized text is sent to or stored on any server — only the amount, currency and category you confirm.

Scan history (optional, based on your consent): if you tick "Save my scan history" (at sign-up or later in Profile), we keep your past scan results so you can see them in the Summary and Calendar screens. You can withdraw this at any time in Profile settings. When you withdraw, past scan records are not deleted but are anonymised: amount, currency and category remain, the date is reduced to the day, and the link to you is removed. Anonymised records can no longer be linked to you and therefore remain after your account is deleted. The App works fully without this option.

Financial profile (optional): if you choose to fill it in, we also store a monthly income range, your daily/monthly work hours, up to 5 recurring monthly expenses, and up to 5 additional income sources. These are used only for in-app spending-context calculations (e.g. "how many work-hours does this cost") and are never required. All of these fields are cleared automatically if you change your country or currency.

IP addresses: our application tables store only a keyed hash of your IP address (for rate limiting), never the raw address. However, our authentication provider (Supabase Auth) keeps a record of each login session that includes the IP address and device/browser information (user agent); it exists for as long as the session exists and is deleted when your account is deleted. Like any hosting provider, Supabase may also process connection IP addresses in its standard technical and security logs under its own retention schedule.

We do not collect: card or bank account numbers (payment runs entirely through Google Play), biometric data, location data, or contacts. The App contains no analytics, crash-reporting or advertising SDKs.

Google Sign-In: if you sign in with Google, Google passes us your name, email address, Google account identifier and profile picture link. These are retained in our authentication provider's (Supabase Auth) technical records but are never read, displayed, or otherwise used by the App; your display name is always the one you typed. The temporary records of the sign-in handshake itself (which can include a Google access token) are deleted automatically within about an hour and a quarter.

5. Who Can See Your Data Inside the App

Shared rooms and joint goals: other members see only your display name or nickname (depending on your setting). With "anonymous mode" on, your nickname is shown instead of your name, and the fact that anonymous mode is on is visible to other members. Your email, subscription and country are never shown to members. The amount you contribute to a joint goal is visible to the other members of that goal. Your personal debts, savings goals and calendar are private to you.

User code: the user code shown in your profile is not currently used for search or adding; it is reserved for a possible future "add friend" feature.

Invite links and codes: before you accept, they show only the room/goal name, currency and status; they do not reveal the creator's identity or the member list.

6. Retention, Deletion and Account Recovery

When you request account deletion, your account is immediately marked for deletion. You can cancel this by simply signing back in within 14 days — this restores your account automatically.

After 14 days, your account is permanently deleted, including your authentication record. Your personal data (profile, debts, goals, reminders) is fully erased. In most cases, records shared with other users (e.g. a contribution in a joint goal) are anonymised rather than deleted, so other members' history is preserved without any personal link to you — except as described below if you created the room or goal.

Important: deleting your account does not cancel your Google Play subscription. You must cancel it separately through Google Play.

If you created a room or joint goal: deleting your account also permanently deletes all other members' records in that room/goal, not just yours. You will see an additional in-app warning before this happens.

Other retention periods: hashed rate-limiting data is deleted automatically, in practice within about an hour. In-app room notifications are deleted automatically after 90 days (or earlier if the room or your account is deleted). When you request account deletion, your device push token is removed immediately. Support requests are kept in our database for at most 1 year. Brevo deletes its transactional email logs after 1 month and never stores email previews; the copy of your message in the developer's own inbox follows ordinary mailbox retention and may be kept longer. Other data is kept only as long as your account exists, or as required by law.

7. Security Measures

We apply technical and organisational measures appropriate to the risk, including: row-level access control at the database level (each user can only access their own or shared data); encrypted session storage on your device; keyed hashing of IP addresses; server-side input validation; hardened payment verification; screenshot/recording protection on screens showing financial data (Android); and a documented, ongoing security review process.
